CH4-024 [PDF 12]
Q: Oxidation and reduction of metals and non-metals
A: 1. The oxidation of metals represents the transition of the atoms into positive ions or the transition of positive ions into positive ions of greater oxidation number

2. The oxidation of non-metals (especially gases) represents the transition of negative ions into molecules except hydrogen which represents the transition of a molecule into positive ions.

3. The reduction of metals represents the transition of positive ions into atoms or the transition of positive ions into positive ions of less oxidation number.

4. The reduction of non-metals represents the transition of the molecules into negative ions except the hydrogen which represents the transition of positive ions into a molecule.

CH4-042 [PDF 19]
Q: What happens when a zinc rod is dipped into copper sulfate solution?
A: 1- the zinc rod starts to corrode and disappear. The concentration of zinc ions increases. That means a spontaneous reaction occurs. The zinc (Zn) atoms are oxidized and transform into ions Zn+2.

2- brownish copper element starts plating zinc rod as a spongy layer. Some of the copper settles at the bottom because the copper ions Cu+2 are reduced and thus transform into copper atoms Cu

3- the blue color of the solution starts to disappear due to the decrease in the concentration of copper ions and finally it becomes colorless.

CH4-048 [PDF 22]
Q: Salt Bridge
A: The salt bridge is a conducting medium turned down U-shaped glass tube inside the tube there is an inert electrolyte solution which does not change chemically during the process. The solution contains ager substance (a natural , sticky substance). (it has different uses when heated it liquefies , it solidifies at room temperature) . Function: It is used to transfer the negative ions from the cathode solution to the anode solution and the positive ions from the anode solution to the cathode solution in order to equalize (balance) the concentrations. The salt bridge represents the internal circuit through which ions transfer. the compounds which are used while filling the salt bridge is K2SO4 , KCl , KNO3
