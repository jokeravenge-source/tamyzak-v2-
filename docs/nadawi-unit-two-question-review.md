# مسابقة الأستاذ محمد النداوي — Unit Two

جميع أسئلة الورقتين: 24 MCQ + 17 إجابة قصيرة من كلمة واحدة + سؤال رسالة مستقل. لم تُحذف أسئلة تحت تعليمات «أجب عن خمسة/عشرة فقط».

هذه نسخة مراجعة للأسئلة والمفاتيح المعدّة للموقع. النتيجة الآلية تحسب عدد الإجابات الصحيحة من 41، وليست توزيع الدرجات الأصلي للورقة. سؤال الرسالة يحتاج تصحيحاً يدوياً.

## Q1.1 · MCQ

Why do police officers have many different duties?

- **A.** Because they work only at airports.
- **B.** Because the law covers many different situations.
- **C.** Because they do not investigate crimes.
- **D.** Because drivers choose their duties.

**Answer:** B. Because the law covers many different situations.

تعدّد القوانين يفرض واجبات متنوّعة على الشرطة.

## Q1.2 · MCQ

How can witnesses help the police?

- **A.** By repairing the police cars.
- **B.** By deciding the prison sentence.
- **C.** By describing the suspect and saying when the crime happened.
- **D.** By replacing fingerprint experts.

**Answer:** C. By describing the suspect and saying when the crime happened.

الشاهد يزوّد الشرطة بمعلومات مثل وصف المجرم ووقت وقوع الجريمة.

## Q1.3 · MCQ

What is a radar speed gun?

- **A.** A device used to measure a moving vehicle's speed.
- **B.** A machine that checks baggage for metal.
- **C.** A device that prints driving licences.
- **D.** A machine that controls traffic lights.

**Answer:** A. A device used to measure a moving vehicle's speed.

جهاز يقيس سرعة المركبة، وليس جهاز كشف المعادن أو إشارة مرور.

## Q1.4 · MCQ

What do some people claim about radar speed guns?

- **A.** That they always prevent every accident.
- **B.** That they cannot be used beside a road.
- **C.** That they work only on parked cars.
- **D.** That an inaccurate reading can lead to an unfair ticket.

**Answer:** D. That an inaccurate reading can lead to an unfair ticket.

هذا ادّعاء بعض الناس عن احتمال الخطأ؛ لا يعني أن كل قراءات الرادار خاطئة.

## Q1.5 · MCQ

Radar guns can only measure the speed of cars moving towards them.

- **A.** True
- **B.** False

**Answer:** B. False

العبارة خطأ؛ يمكن قياس سيارة تقترب أو تبتعد عن الجهاز.

## Q1.6 · MCQ

Which pair gives two duties of police officers?

- **A.** Designing cars and selling tickets for flights.
- **B.** Teaching every school subject and treating all illnesses.
- **C.** Controlling road traffic and preventing offences.
- **D.** Repairing aircraft and printing newspapers.

**Answer:** C. Controlling road traffic and preventing offences.

تنظيم المرور ومنع الجرائم من واجبات الشرطة.

## Q2A.1 · MCQ

Choose the correct rewrite expressing expectation: You like exercising, so I don't think you'll have trouble getting fit.

- **A.** You like exercising, so you should having trouble getting fit.
- **B.** You like exercising, so you shouldn't have trouble getting fit.
- **C.** You like exercising, so you shouldn't to have trouble getting fit.
- **D.** You like exercising, so you should has trouble getting fit.

**Answer:** B. You like exercising, so you shouldn't have trouble getting fit.

نستعمل shouldn't + الفعل المجرّد لتوقّع عدم حصول مشكلة.

## Q2A.2 · MCQ

We use ___ to say that it is important not to do something.

- **A.** don't have to
- **B.** needn't
- **C.** mustn't
- **D.** may

**Answer:** C. mustn't

mustn't تفيد المنع، بينما don't have to وneedn't تفيدان عدم الضرورة.

## Q2A.3 · MCQ

Ahmed is travelling tomorrow. Give him advice using 'have your baggage with you'.

- **A.** You should have your baggage with you.
- **B.** You should had your baggage with you.
- **C.** You should to have your baggage with you.
- **D.** You should having your baggage with you.

**Answer:** A. You should have your baggage with you.

بعد should يأتي الفعل بصيغته المجرّدة: have.

## Q2A.4 · MCQ

Choose the correct rewrite using 'make': The manager told me to send the e-mail.

- **A.** The manager made me to send the e-mail.
- **B.** The manager made me sent the e-mail.
- **C.** The manager made me sending the e-mail.
- **D.** The manager made me send the e-mail.

**Answer:** D. The manager made me send the e-mail.

صيغة الماضي السببية: made + مفعول به + فعل مجرّد.

## Q2A.5 · MCQ

Which sentence offers your friend a cup of tea?

- **A.** Would you get me a cup of tea?
- **B.** Would you like a cup of tea?
- **C.** You must give me a cup of tea.
- **D.** Did you drink a cup of tea?

**Answer:** B. Would you like a cup of tea?

Would you like ...? هنا عرض شيء للصديق، لا طلبه منه.

## Q2A.6 · MCQ

Choose the polite request using 'Would': Get me a drink of water.

- **A.** Would you getting me a drink of water?
- **B.** Would you to get me a drink of water?
- **C.** Would you get me a drink of water, please?
- **D.** Would you got me a drink of water?

**Answer:** C. Would you get me a drink of water, please?

الطلب المؤدّب: Would you + فعل مجرّد.

## Q2A.7 · MCQ

Choose the correct expectation: This is a new computer, so ___.

- **A.** it should be faster than the other one
- **B.** it should to be faster than the other one
- **C.** it should being faster than the other one
- **D.** it should is faster than the other one

**Answer:** A. it should be faster than the other one

should be تستعمل للتوقّع، ويتبع should فعل مجرّد.

## Q2A.8 · MCQ

Rewrite using a causative verb: They arrived late because of the traffic jam.

- **A.** The traffic jam made them to arrive late.
- **B.** The traffic jam made them arrived late.
- **C.** The traffic jam made them arriving late.
- **D.** The traffic jam made them arrive late.

**Answer:** D. The traffic jam made them arrive late.

made them arrive: لا نضيف to بعد made في هذا التركيب.

## Q2A.9 · MCQ

Make a suggestion using 'we could': We watch the film.

- **A.** We could watching the film.
- **B.** We could watch the film.
- **C.** We could to watch the film.
- **D.** We could watched the film.

**Answer:** B. We could watch the film.

بعد could نستخدم watch من دون to أو تصريف.

## Q2A.10 · MCQ

At our school, we must ___ a uniform.

- **A.** wore
- **B.** wearing
- **C.** wear
- **D.** worn

**Answer:** C. wear

must يتبعها الفعل المجرّد wear.

## Q2A.11 · MCQ

Which sentence means that a specialist repaired my camera for me?

- **A.** I had my camera repaired.
- **B.** I had my camera repair.
- **C.** I made my camera repaired.
- **D.** I had my camera repairing.

**Answer:** A. I had my camera repaired.

have + مفعول به + تصريف ثالث، والفعل بالماضي هنا had.

## Q2A.12 · MCQ

Ask about people's necessity to follow the law using 'must'.

- **A.** Do people must follow the law?
- **B.** Must people to follow the law?
- **C.** Must people following the law?
- **D.** Must people follow the law?

**Answer:** D. Must people follow the law?

في السؤال يتقدّم must على الفاعل ويتبعه الفعل المجرّد.

## Q2B.1 · MCQ

You ___ pick me up in the car. I'll get the bus.

- **A.** have to
- **B.** needn't

**Answer:** B. needn't

استخدام الحافلة يعني أن التوصيل غير ضروري: needn't.

## Q2B.2 · MCQ

You ___ ask a neighbour to collect mail while you are away.

- **A.** should
- **B.** shouldn't

**Answer:** A. should

هذه نصيحة مفيدة أثناء السفر، فنستخدم should.

## Q2B.3 · MCQ

I ___ to leave so soon.

- **A.** needn't
- **B.** don't have

**Answer:** B. don't have

نقول don't have to. أمّا needn't فيأتي بعدها الفعل من دون to.

## Q2B.4 · MCQ

___ pick you up and take you to the office?

- **A.** Would you like
- **B.** Would you like me to

**Answer:** B. Would you like me to

العرض للقيام بفعل: Would you like me to + فعل.

## Q2B.5 · MCQ

He learnt about computers in the military. He ___ be able to help me fix mine.

- **A.** should
- **B.** shouldn't

**Answer:** A. should

معرفته بالحاسوب سبب لتوقّع قدرته على المساعدة.

## Q2B.6 · MCQ

He had his house ___ into when he was on a holiday.

- **A.** broke
- **B.** broken

**Answer:** B. broken

بعد had his house نحتاج التصريف الثالث broken.

Don't leave ___ in the car. (valuable possessions)

Word box: essential · powder · solid · arrest · base · valuables


**Answer:** valuables

valuables اسم جمع يدل على الأشياء الثمينة.

## Q3A.3 · One-word answer

The police use special ___ to see fingerprints.

Word box: essential · powder · solid · arrest · base · valuables


**Answer:** powder

الإجابة الأصلية special powder؛ أُعطيت special حتى تكون الكتابة كلمة واحدة.

## Q3A.4 · One-word answer

Officers can be doctors at the army ___.

Word box: essential · powder · solid · arrest · base · valuables


**Answer:** base

army base تعني قاعدة عسكرية.

## Q3A.5 · One-word answer

Make sure your door is ___. (strong and firm)

Word box: essential · powder · solid · arrest · base · valuables


**Answer:** solid

solid تصف الباب المتين؛ التلميح يحدّد مفردة السؤال الأصلي.

## Q3B.1 · One-word answer

arrest = put someone in prison; dispose of = get ___ of.


**Answer:** rid

get rid of هو معنى dispose of؛ بقيت كلمة rid وحدها للكتابة.

## Q3B.2 · One-word answer

On the phone, introduce yourself: '___ is Samir Esam.'


**Answer:** this

عند التعريف بنفسك على الهاتف نستخدم This is ...؛ أُضيف سياق حوار الكتاب لرفع الغموض.

## Q3B.3 · One-word answer

Avoid ___ in illegal places. (leaving your car)


**Answer:** parking

بعد avoid نستعمل صيغة -ing: parking.

## Q3B.4 · One-word answer

In the military, you can take a test to become an ___. (officer / official)


**Answer:** officer

officer تعني ضابطاً؛ official تعني مسؤولاً ولا تعطي المعنى المطلوب هنا.

## Q3B.5 · One-word answer

Your car mustn't ___ broken lights.


**Answer:** have

بعد mustn't يأتي الفعل المجرّد have.

## Q3B.6 · One-word answer

Put your bags on the ___ belt, please. (conveyor / seat)


**Answer:** conveyor

الإجابة الأصلية conveyor belt؛ كلمة belt معطاة حتى تبقى الإجابة كلمة واحدة.

## Q3C.1 · One-word answer

Write the abbreviation of 'frequent'.


**Answer:** freq.

الاختصار freq.؛ تقبل الإجابة مع النقطة النهائية أو من دونها.

## Q3C.2 · One-word answer

play → player; teach → ___


**Answer:** teacher

اسم الشخص الذي يدرّس: teacher.

## Q3C.3 · One-word answer

The opposite of 'military' is ___.


**Answer:** civilian

civilian تعني مدني، وهي مقابل military.

## Q3C.4 · One-word answer

hurt → injured; called → ___


**Answer:** named (also accepted: alerted, per competition correction)

called وnamed مترادفتان بمعنى مُسمّى.

## Q3C.5 · One-word answer

Engl. → English; driving lic. → driving ___ (British English)


**Answer:** licence

الصيغة البريطانية للاسم هي licence؛ كلمة driving معطاة.

## Q3C.6 · One-word answer

radar speed → gun; speeding → ___


**Answer:** ticket (also accepted: speeding ticket)

التركيب المطلوب speeding ticket؛ يُقبل ticket أو speeding ticket، ولا يُقبل fine.
